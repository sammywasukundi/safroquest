FAQ

Do I need a wallet?

No. You can create a SafroQuest wallet in the app. You can also connect an existing wallet.

Is my secret phrase stored on a server?

No. It is generated and encrypted on your device. If you lose both the phrase and your device, nobody can recover it, so write it down on paper.

What is on the blockchain today?

The V1 uses a local server for XP and questions. Rank badges and the on-chain registry (CosmWasm) come in the next version.

Is this an official Safrochain product?

No. It is an independent community project, proposed to the Safrochain ecosystem.
